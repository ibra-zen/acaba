Summary: Am I Stupid? is an offline game. We do not collect, store, sell, or transmit any personal data. All game progress is kept strictly on your local device.

1. Introduction

Welcome to Am I Stupid? ("the Application"). We are dedicated to respecting and safeguarding your privacy. This policy details our handling of user data.

2. Information Collection & Usage

We do not collect any personal data:

  • No Personal Identifiers: We do not ask for or collect names, email addresses, phone numbers, or account registrations.
  • No Device Permissions: We do not access camera, microphone, GPS location, contacts, or storage files.
  • Local Storage Only: Your score, current level, and nickname are stored purely on your device (`localStorage`) and never leave it.

3. Third-Party Services

The Application contains no third-party tracking tools, analytics SDKs, or advertising networks.

4. Children's Privacy (COPPA)

The Application is family-friendly and does not collect information from anyone, including children under the age of 13.

5. Data Control & Deletion

You can delete your data at any time by resetting your progress in the game or clearing the app cache in Android Settings.

6. Contact

If you have any questions, please open an issue on our GitHub repository: github.com/ibra-zen/acaba.
